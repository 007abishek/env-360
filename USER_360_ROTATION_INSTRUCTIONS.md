# User Instructions: How to Rotate for Full 360 Coverage

Use these steps while scanning so the app can capture a complete 360-degree environment view.

## Before You Start

- Hold the phone in straight portrait position.
- Keep the phone perpendicular to the floor and walls.
- Point the camera at the room, not too far up or down.
- Stand in one spot if possible.

## How to Rotate

1. Tap **Start Scan**.
2. Begin turning in one direction only, either left or right.
3. Rotate slowly and smoothly.
4. Follow the on-screen 3D arrow guide.
5. Keep turning until coverage reaches **100%**.
6. When full coverage is complete, tap **Generate Panorama**.

## Important Rules

- Do not rotate back and forth.
- Do not reverse direction before the full 360 is complete.
- Do not move too fast.
- Do not tilt the phone sideways.
- Do not walk around while rotating unless absolutely necessary.

## Good Scanning Technique

- Turn your body gradually instead of twisting only your wrist.
- Pause as little as possible during the sweep.
- Try to cover the room evenly as you turn.
- Make one continuous full circle.

## If Coverage Is Not Increasing

- Straighten the phone in portrait mode.
- Return to a steady speed.
- Aim the camera at a new part of the room.
- Keep rotating in the same direction.
- Restart the scan if the app reports timeout or direction reversal.

## Quick Summary

- Start scan
- Hold phone straight
- Rotate slowly
- Stay in one direction
- Reach 100% coverage
- Generate panorama
